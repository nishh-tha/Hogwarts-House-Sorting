About This Experience

The legend behind the ceremony

Every witch or wizard has a place at Hogwarts — but only the Sorting Hat can truly reveal where you belong.

This experience recreates the legendary sorting ceremony in a modern, interactive form. Through a series of magical scenarios, your choices shape a path that reflects your deepest traits, values, and instincts.

Bravery. Ambition. Wisdom. Loyalty.

Choose wisely — for the Hat sees not only who you are, but who you have the potential to become.
